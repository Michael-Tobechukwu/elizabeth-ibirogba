God Is Intentional About You

Have you ever looked back on a season of your life and wondered why it had to unfold that way?

Perhaps you are in a season where the waiting, the silence, the paths seem to lead nowhere.

God Is Intentional About You is written for those quiet moments of reflection. It unfolds like a sacred conversation, presenting each chapter as a gentle reminder that God has never been distant, careless, or unaware.

Page after page, it reveals a God who chose you on purpose, who entrusted you with spiritual authority, who watches over you at your duty post, and who strengthens you to endure without bowing under pressure. Nothing about your journey has been accidental, every lesson, every pause, every test has carried intention.

If you have ever questioned whether God sees you, remembers you, or still has a plan for you, this book is an invitation to rediscover the deliberate love of a God who has been intentional about you from the very beginning.
